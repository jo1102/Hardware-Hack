    On the box

    Exactly what the patient sees on the 1602 screen — 16 characters by 2 lines.

    HD44780 · 16×2 · I²C 0x27 · SDA 14 / SCL 42
    Stock warnings never appear on the box. Running low is your job, not theirs — it surfaces at the top of this page instead.
    A green dot is not proof of a servo. Nothing on the board can sense whether one is wired to a pin — it only confirms the PWM channel came up. Use Test gate on a tube card to check the mechanism.

      Wellbeing check-in

      Live view from the XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense beside the box.

      ◉

      Camera is off

      The stream stays off until a carer asks for it. Enter the camera address and press Start check-in.

      🔒

      This is not monitoring. The camera has no recording, no storage and no face recognition. It opens only when a carer presses the button, shows an indicator while it is on, and closes when you leave this page — a consented look-in to decide whether somebody needs a phone call, nothing more.

      Camera

      Second board, its own WiFi connection.

      Streamidle
      BoardXIAO ESP32-S3 Sense
      FeedMJPEG :81/stream
      The address changes every boot — it comes from DHCP. Rather than reading it off the serial log each time, press Find it for me: the bridge sweeps the network you are both on and looks for the camera. That works even when the XIAO is running off breadboard power with no cable attached.
      The sketch needs PSRAM=opi at compile time, and Start Stream has to be pressed once on the camera's own page — it does not auto-start.

      Reminder sound

      What the box plays when a dose is due. Stored on the board, so it survives a power cut.

      35%
      Loud enough matters. The chime has to carry into the next room, but it also plays several times a day for years — anything that reads as an emergency in week one gets ignored by week three. That is why none of these are alarms.
      A missed dose or an empty tube always uses a separate, more insistent tune so the two can never be confused. That one is deliberately not adjustable.
